How this brief was made. Problem Radar searched {{ m.sources }} public sources. {{ m.model }} proposed problems from them; Python then checked every quote word for word, required two independent websites, and calculated each score as 20 × (0.30 severity + 0.20 frequency + 0.20 market + 0.15 willingness to pay + 0.15 competition gap). The insights and verdict were written by Python from those checks, not by the AI.

This is a starting point for customer research, not a market study. Open the sources in the full report before making decisions.
